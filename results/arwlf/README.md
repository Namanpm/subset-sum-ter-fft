# `results/arwlf/`

**What it is.** Evidence for the ARWLF article: campaigns, solves, comparisons, snapshots and the ASan record.

**Purpose in the repository.** Everything in the paper's evidence chain that is raw output lives here. The frozen, hash-checked copies the paper reads are in `paper/data/`. `holdout-snapshots/` holds 2.9 GB of untracked `.snap` files; `history/` holds superseded runs. `hash-join-n108-anchor.log` is the hash-join baseline output.
